# Code of conduct

slopguard is a small project that depends on people being able to disagree about code without it
becoming personal. This is how we expect everyone to behave in issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other space connected to the project.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Most problems are misunderstandings. Ask before you accuse.
- **Be specific and generous in review.** Criticise code, not people. Say what is good as well as what to change.
- **Welcome newcomers.** Answer the "obvious" question patiently. Everyone was new once.
- **Report problems with evidence.** A false positive with a small reproducing example is a gift, not a complaint.
- **Respect people's time and privacy.** Do not post other people's personal information or private messages.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, threats, or demeaning comments, including jokes that target a person or group.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, stalking, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors, or repeating a request after a clear "no".
- Anything that would be unacceptable in a professional workplace.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately by opening a **private security advisory** on this repository
(Security tab, "Report a vulnerability"), marked "conduct", or by contacting the maintainer
[@patsypppe](https://github.com/patsypppe) through their GitHub profile. Reports are read by the
maintainers only and handled confidentially.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not follow this
code. Minor first issues usually get a private reminder. Serious or repeated ones get removal from the
project's spaces. We will tell you what was decided.

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
It was written for this project in plain language, with ideas from widely used community codes such as the
Contributor Covenant.
